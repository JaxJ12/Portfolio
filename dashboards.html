03 — Workflow Automation

Alpha & Omega Transcription Tool

A real workflow tool built for a family business — not a demo. Insurance agents paste a call transcript, and the tool automatically extracts client details, surfaces relevant plans and pricing, generates a step-by-step action list, drafts a follow-up email, and optionally sends it on the spot. Built entirely with AI to solve an actual daily pain point.

Workflow Automation AI Processing Email Automation AI-Built Real-World Use

How it works

1

Agent finishes a client call

They paste in their raw notes or spoken transcript — no formatting required.

2

AI parses the full conversation

Extracts client details, Medicare or life insurance plans mentioned, pricing discussed, urgency signals, and next-contact timing.

3

Full automation package delivered

A task checklist, step-by-step guide, and a ready-to-send follow-up email — generated in seconds, no manual writeup needed.
