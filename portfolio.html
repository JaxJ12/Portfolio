A cleaner way to navigate the portfolio

The overview now acts like a front door instead of a folder of links. The featured preview gives a quick read on tone and substance, while the rail helps different viewers self-select the route that matters most to them.

  • Recruiter route: start with Experience, then About, then Social for proof.
  • Brand route: open Social first, then Experience for credibility and breadth.
  • AI route: go from Dashboards to Projects to Chatbot for the clearest build story.
  • Professor or reviewer route: Projects first, then the supporting pages that show context and communication range.
